How it works

  1. Pay securely

    Checkout is hosted by Stripe. Card details go to Stripe; we never see them.

  2. Receive your key

    Your licence key arrives by email within a few minutes of paying.

  3. Activate and work offline

    Paste the key into Camog (one-time internet check). After that, Camog runs fully offline for the licence term.

Common questions

What happens when the licence expires?

Camog becomes read-only: existing records stay viewable, capture and editing pause. Buy again, activate the new key, and everything continues. Your records are never touched. If you subscribe and leave auto-renew switched on in Settings → Licence, the renewed licence installs itself after each payment — nothing to re-enter.

What counts as a seat, and can I move one?

A seat is one activated computer. Replacing a computer is free: email us and we free the old seat for the new one.

What currency are prices in?

All prices are in Australian dollars (AUD). Stripe's receipt is your invoice.
