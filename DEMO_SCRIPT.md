# Demo Script: Camog — Clinical Photo Documentation

**Target runtime**: ~55 seconds (≈140 spoken words)
**Format**: screen recording, single take or quick cuts per step

## Setup
- App running (`npm run desktop` or the installed build), sitting on the passcode login screen.
- A demo patient (e.g. "Jane Doe") pre-loaded with a few photos so the timeline, library, and report have content.
- Webcam connected; phone with the camera page open for the QR pairing shot.

---

## Step 1: Hook — title card (0:00–0:05)
**What to show**: Camog logo over a blurred glimpse of the photo library.
**Talking points**: "Clinical photos, documented properly — without a single byte leaving your clinic."

---

## Step 2: Passcode login & auto-lock (0:05–0:12)
**What to show**: Type the passcode to unlock; briefly show the idle lock overlay kicking in.
**Talking points**: "Every session starts behind a passcode, and it auto-locks when you step away."

---

## Step 3: Capture — webcam, phone QR, body map (0:12–0:21)
**What to show**: Open Capture, webcam live; click "use phone", show QR code, then a phone snapping a shot; tap the body map to tag the body part.
**Talking points**: "Capture opens your webcam — or scan a QR code to turn your phone into the camera. Tap the body map to tag exactly where it was taken."

---

## Step 4: Dashboard at a glance (0:21–0:28)
**What to show**: Dashboard with stats cards, activity calendar, and the charts.
**Talking points**: "The dashboard keeps score: patients, photos, and activity — at a glance."

---

## Step 5: Patients & photo timeline (0:28–0:35)
**What to show**: Search for Jane Doe in Patients, open her record, scroll the photo timeline.
**Talking points**: "Search patients, open a record, and see every photo on one timeline."

---

## Step 6: Photo library — zoom, annotate, compare (0:35–0:44)
**What to show**: Open Photos, zoom into a shot, draw an annotation, then open the before/after compare view.
**Talking points**: "In the library, zoom in, annotate findings, and compare before-and-after side by side."

---

## Step 7: One-click case report (0:44–0:50)
**What to show**: Generate Jane's case report and flash the finished printable page.
**Talking points**: "When it's time to share, generate a clean case report in one click."

---

## Step 8: Settings — trust & control (0:50–0:55)
**What to show**: Quick sweep across Settings panels: Users, Audit Log, Backup, Storage.
**Talking points**: "Add users, review the audit log, back up, and store photos anywhere — even a synced cloud folder. Everything stays local and works offline."

---

## Wrap-up
**Key takeaways**:
- Camog documents clinical photos with patient and body-part metadata — and the data never leaves the machine.
- Capture from webcam or phone, organise on a body map, annotate and compare, and produce case reports.
- Practice-ready controls: passcode + auto-lock, user invites, audit log, backups, flexible storage, offline licences.

**Next steps**: "Camog. Clinical photo documentation that stays on your side of the wall." — direct viewers to try it (14-day trial starts on first launch).
