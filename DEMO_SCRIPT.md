# Demo Script: Camog — Clinical Photo Documentation

**Target runtime**: ~55 seconds (≈145 spoken words) · v0.3.0
**Format**: screen recording, single take or quick cuts per step

## Setup
- App running (`npm run desktop` or the installed build), sitting on the passcode login screen — with branding applied so the login already shows the practice's logo and colours.
- A demo patient (e.g. "Jane Doe") pre-loaded with a few photos so the timeline, library, and report have content — and one patient with an overdue review so the Needs Attention panel has a row.
- Webcam connected; phone ready to pair for the companion shot.

---

## Step 1: Hook — title card (0:00–0:05)
**What to show**: Camog logo over a blurred glimpse of the branded photo library.
**Talking points**: "Clinical photos, documented properly — without a single byte leaving your clinic."

---

## Step 2: Passcode login & auto-lock (0:05–0:11)
**What to show**: Type the passcode to unlock; briefly show the idle lock overlay kicking in.
**Talking points**: "Every session starts behind a passcode, and it auto-locks when you step away."

---

## Step 3: Capture — webcam, paired phone, body map (0:11–0:20)
**What to show**: Open Capture, webcam live; start the phone link, scan the QR once, then snap from the phone — the pairing survives navigating pages; tap the body map to tag the body part.
**Talking points**: "Capture from your webcam — or pair your phone once and shoot from it, with optional access to the shared library. Tap the body map to tag exactly where."

---

## Step 4: Dashboard — stats + what needs attention (0:20–0:27)
**What to show**: Stats cards, then the Needs Attention panel (overdue review, expired consent) and the Recent Actions feed.
**Talking points**: "The dashboard keeps score — and flags what needs your eye: overdue reviews, expired consent, plus a live activity feed."

---

## Step 5: Patients — review badges & timeline (0:27–0:34)
**What to show**: Search for Jane Doe in Patients; point out the review-status badges in the list; open her record and scroll the photo timeline.
**Talking points**: "Search patients — review badges show who's due or overdue — and see every photo on one timeline."

---

## Step 6: Photo library — zoom, annotate, compare (0:34–0:42)
**What to show**: Open Photos, zoom into a shot, draw an annotation, then open the before/after compare view.
**Talking points**: "In the library, zoom in, annotate findings, and compare before-and-after side by side."

---

## Step 7: One-click case report (0:42–0:48)
**What to show**: Generate Jane's case report and flash the finished printable page.
**Talking points**: "When it's time to share, generate a clean case report in one click."

---

## Step 8: Settings — control, trust, and your brand (0:48–0:55)
**What to show**: Sweep Settings: Users & invites, Audit Log, Backup, Storage — then the Branding editor with live logo and colour pickers.
**Talking points**: "Manage users, audit, backups, and storage — and make it yours: your logo, your colours, on every screen. All local, all offline."

---

## Wrap-up
**Key takeaways**:
- Camog documents clinical photos with patient and body-part metadata — and the data never leaves the machine.
- Capture from webcam or a paired phone, track reviews and consent, annotate and compare, and produce one-click case reports.
- Practice-ready controls: passcode + auto-lock, user invites, audit log, backups, flexible storage, custom branding, offline licences.

**Next steps**: "Camog. Clinical photo documentation that stays on your side of the wall." — direct viewers to try it (14-day trial starts on first launch).
